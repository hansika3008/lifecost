Why Subscriptions Add Up

A monthly price can look small on its own, but the total grows as the subscription continues.

Think in months and years

A ₹499 monthly subscription costs ₹5,988 over 12 months before considering taxes or price changes.

Estimate your usage

When you know roughly how many times you use a service each month, you can estimate an effective cost per use.

Check recurring services

Review subscriptions periodically and compare the amount you pay with how often you actually use the service.

Treat the result as an estimate

Prices, taxes, promotions and usage can change, so a calculation is a planning aid rather than a guarantee of future spending.
