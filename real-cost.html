What Counts as Real Cost?

The price on a product page is only one part of what an item can cost over time.

Start with the purchase price

The purchase price is the starting point, but it may not represent the full amount you spend during ownership.

Add ongoing costs

Maintenance, repairs, accessories, service fees and other recurring expenses can increase the total cost over the useful life of an item.

Consider resale value

An item that can be resold may return some value at the end of ownership. Subtracting an estimated resale value can help produce a net-cost estimate.

Remember other factors

Taxes, financing costs, opportunity cost and changes in your actual usage can affect the real-world result. These are reasons to treat a calculator result as an estimate.
