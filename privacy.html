Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LifeCost is a browser-based tool designed to help users estimate the long-term cost of purchases and subscriptions.

Information You Enter

Information entered into LifeCost's calculators is used to perform the calculations requested by you. The calculator is designed to process these calculations directly in your browser.

Browser Storage

LifeCost may use browser storage, such as local storage, to support website functionality or remember information locally. Information stored this way remains on your device unless the website's functionality specifically states otherwise.

Cookies and Advertising

LifeCost may use cookies or similar technologies when third-party services such as advertising or analytics are enabled.

If Google advertising services are used, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies to help serve advertisements based on a user's visits to this website or other websites.

Users may be able to manage or opt out of personalized advertising through Google's Ads Settings or other available advertising preference tools.

Third-Party Services

LifeCost may use third-party services such as analytics, advertising, hosting or other website-support services. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Data Security

LifeCost does not ask users to create an account in order to use the calculator. The calculator is designed to keep calculation information in the user's browser.

Children

LifeCost is a general-purpose website and is not specifically directed toward children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when LifeCost's functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change.
