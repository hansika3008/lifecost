Why real cost matters

A ₹60,000 product used hundreds of times can have a very different effective cost from a ₹3,000 product used only a few times. LifeCost separates the upfront price from the way you actually use an item.

The calculator is an estimate. Taxes, financing, repairs, opportunity cost, replacement timing and changing resale values can affect the real-world result.
