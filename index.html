LEARN

Why real cost matters

A purchase has more than an upfront price. Its lifespan, usage, maintenance and resale value can change the effective cost over time.

LifeCost provides estimates. Taxes, financing, opportunity cost, unexpected repairs, replacement timing and changing resale values can affect the real-world result.

LIFECOST GUIDES

Useful ways to think about everyday purchases.

LifeCost turns simple estimates into numbers you can use when researching a purchase.

How to calculate cost per use

Take the estimated net cost of an item and divide it by the number of times you expect to use it. For example, an item used frequently can have a lower cost per use even when its upfront price is higher.

What counts as real cost?

LifeCost can include the purchase price, expected maintenance and expected resale value. Other factors such as financing, taxes, repairs and opportunity cost may also matter in a real purchase.

How to compare two products

Compare the same metrics for both products: net cost, cost per use, lifespan and expected usage. Then consider features, quality, warranty and your personal requirements separately.

Why subscriptions add up

A small monthly subscription becomes a larger amount over many months. LifeCost's subscription tool helps turn a monthly price into a longer-term total and, when usage is provided, an estimated cost per use.
