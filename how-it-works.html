How LifeCost Works

LifeCost estimates the long-term cost of a purchase using the information you provide.

1. Purchase Price

Enter the amount you paid or expect to pay for the product.

2. Expected Life

Enter how many years you expect to use the product.

3. Uses Per Week

Enter approximately how many times you expect to use the product each week.

4. Maintenance

Enter the estimated maintenance cost per year.

5. Expected Resale

Enter the estimated amount you may receive when reselling the product.

What LifeCost Shows

  • Net cost over the expected lifetime
  • Cost per use
  • Cost per day
  • Cost per month
  • Average cost per year
  • Estimated total uses

These figures are estimates and depend on the values entered by the user. They should be used as a planning tool rather than a guarantee of future costs.
