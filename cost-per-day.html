How to Calculate Cost Per Day

Cost per day gives you a simple way to understand how much a purchase costs across the period you expect to own it.

What is cost per day?

Cost per day is the estimated net cost of an item divided by the number of days in its expected ownership period.

The basic formula

The basic formula is:

Cost per day = Net cost ÷ Total ownership days

For a simple example, suppose the net cost of an item is ₹36,500 and you expect to own it for one year.

₹36,500 ÷ 365 = ₹100 per day.

What should be included in net cost?

Depending on the purchase, net cost can include the original price, expected maintenance during ownership and other ownership expenses, minus an estimated resale value.

Why cost per day can be useful

A daily figure can make a large upfront price easier to understand over time. It can also help you compare purchases with different lifespans.

Remember that it is an estimate

Actual costs can change because of repairs, taxes, financing, changing resale values, shorter or longer ownership and changes in how often you use the item.
