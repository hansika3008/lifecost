About LifeCost

LifeCost is a free tool designed to help you understand the real cost of the things you buy.

Instead of looking only at the purchase price, LifeCost considers factors such as expected lifetime, usage, maintenance costs and expected resale value.

Why LifeCost?

The price shown on a product is only one part of its cost. A product that costs more initially may have a different cost over its useful lifetime depending on how often it is used, maintained and resold.

LifeCost turns these inputs into simple estimates such as cost per use, cost per day, cost per month and average cost per year.

All calculations are estimates based on the information entered by the user.
